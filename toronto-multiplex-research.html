Policy Timeline

May 2023
Fourplexes Permitted Citywide
City Council adopts OPA 649 and Zoning By-law Amendment allowing up to 4 units as-of-right in RD, RS, RT zones (~70% of residential land).
November 2024
Ontario Regulation 462/24
Provincial regulation removes FSI limits for lots with secondary suites, increases lot coverage to 45–60%, and aligns standards for duplexes/triplexes.
June 2025
Sixplexes Approved in Select Wards
City Council adopts By-law 654-2025 permitting fiveplexes and sixplexes in Toronto & East York and Ward 23. DCs waived for up to 6 units.
August 2025
EHON Policy Updated
Updated Expanding Housing Options in Neighbourhoods policy enables up to 6 units as-of-right. Permits exceed 600 units issued — a 50% increase from 2024.
2026
Monitoring & Expansion
City Planning reports on multiplex implementation. Cash flows up +55% YoY. Study underway to expand sixplex permissions citywide.
